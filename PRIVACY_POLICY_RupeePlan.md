# Privacy Policy for RupeePlan

**Last Updated:** June 11, 2026

RupeePlan ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the RupeePlan mobile application (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information
We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when registering or logging into the App:
- **Mobile Number:** If you choose to log in via your mobile number, we collect and use your phone number solely to send a One-Time Password (OTP) via SMS for secure authentication.
- **Google Account Profile:** If you choose to sign in using "Continue with Google," we receive basic profile information (such as your name, email address, and profile picture) from Google to establish your account.

### B. App Data and Calculation History
- **Offline Calculation History:** RupeePlan is designed with an offline-first architecture. Any calculations you save (such as SIP, Lumpsum, SWP, EMI, FD, RD, PPF, SSY, NPS, APY, GST, or Income Tax computations) are stored locally on your device using encrypted/secure local storage database boxes.
- **Cloud Synchronization:** If you log in to your account, your saved calculations may be securely backed up and synchronized to our cloud servers so you can access them across multiple devices. If you use the App in **Guest Mode**, your calculation data remains strictly on your local device and is never uploaded to our servers.

---

## 2. Device Permissions We Request

To provide a premium and automated financial planning experience, the App may request access to the following device features:
- **Notifications:** We request permission to send you push notifications to alert you of monthly SIP investment dates, upcoming loan EMI due dates, and other system alerts. You can opt-out of notifications at any time in your device settings.
- **Secure Local Storage:** We require read/write access to a isolated directory on your device to save, rename, and manage your calculation history offline.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
- To authenticate your identity and facilitate secure logins.
- To store, retrieve, and sync your calculation history.
- To deliver notification reminders for your saving and loan plans.
- To maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App's features and user experience.

---

## 4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:
- **Service Providers:** We may share data with trusted third-party services (such as Firebase Authentication or SMS gateway providers) strictly for authentication and operational purposes.
- **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

---

## 5. Security of Your Data

The security of your data is of utmost importance to us. 
- All local database operations are confined to the secure application sandbox on your device.
- All network communications (such as OTP verification or cloud sync) are encrypted using secure protocols (HTTPS/TLS).
- While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

---

## 6. Data Deletion and Control

You have full control over your data:
- **Local History Deletion:** You can delete individual calculations or clear your entire local history directly from the "Saved Plans" screen within the App at any time.
- **Account Deletion:** If you wish to delete your cloud account and all associated synchronized data, you can request account deletion by contacting support.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
