# Privacy Policy for AgriMarket Pro

**Last Updated:** November 21, 2026  
**Effective Date:** November 21, 2026  

Welcome to **AgriMarket Pro** ("we," "our," or "us"), owned and operated for agricultural communities and market participants. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and protected when you use our mobile application **AgriMarket Pro** (Package Name: `com.hkn.hosahalli.mandi`), available on the Google Play Store.

By installing or using AgriMarket Pro, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

AgriMarket Pro is built with a privacy-first approach. We do not require users to create an account, register, or provide personally identifiable information (such as your full name, email address, or phone number) to browse agricultural market prices, view charts, or check weather forecasts.

### A. Information Collected Automatically
* **Device and Usage Information:** Basic non-identifying technical data such as device model, operating system version, app version, language preference, and error/crash diagnostics may be processed temporarily to ensure application stability and proper rendering.

### B. Sensitive Permissions & Device Data
We only request device permissions when strictly necessary to provide core application features:

#### 1. Location Data (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`, `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`)
* **Purpose:** To calculate distances to nearby agricultural produce market committees (APMC Mandis) and to provide localized, real-time weather forecasts and rainfall advisories.
* **Usage:** Location data is accessed **only in the foreground** while the app is active and with your explicit runtime permission. 
* **Storage & Sharing:** Your GPS coordinates are processed on your device and sent ephemerally to weather API endpoints to fetch localized forecasts. Your location is **never sold, shared with advertisers, or stored on our servers to track your movements**.

#### 2. Microphone & Audio (`RECORD_AUDIO`)
* **Purpose:** To enable hands-free voice search for agricultural commodities (e.g., searching for "Wheat", "Tomato", "Arecanut" via speech recognition).
* **Usage:** Audio input is accessed only when you tap the microphone icon.
* **Storage & Sharing:** Audio is converted to search text in real time on the device or via system speech services. We do **not** record, store, or transmit your voice recordings to external servers.

#### 3. Network Access (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`)
* **Purpose:** Required to fetch up-to-date commodity prices, mandi arrivals, MSP details, and weather information, as well as to check network connectivity status for low-bandwidth mode.

---

## 2. On-Device Storage and Preferences

AgriMarket Pro uses local device storage (Hive database) to deliver an optimized and offline-capable experience:
* **User Preferences:** Selected language, metric units (e.g., quintal/kg), and theme settings (dark/light mode).
* **Watchlist & Price Alerts:** Your pinned commodities and target price thresholds.
* **Cached Market Data:** Daily commodity prices are cached locally on your device to enable offline access in rural areas with low network connectivity.

*None of this local data is uploaded to any private database linked to your identity.*

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

To provide reliable market intelligence and weather services, AgriMarket Pro integrates with trusted third-party providers. These third-party services may collect information used to identify your device in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

1. **Google Play Services & Firebase (Google LLC)**
   * Used for backend data synchronization, infrastructure, and app delivery.
   * [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
2. **Open-Meteo API**
   * Used for localized weather forecasting, precipitation predictions, and agricultural advisory data.
   * [Open-Meteo Privacy Policy](https://open-meteo.com/en/features#privacy)
3. **Agmarknet / Open Government Data Platform**
   * Used as the public data source for daily APMC mandi prices and commodity arrivals across Indian markets.

---

## 4. How We Use Your Information

The limited information processed by AgriMarket Pro is used solely to:
* Provide real-time and historical commodity price data.
* Calculate distances to nearest Mandis.
* Deliver localized agro-weather forecasts.
* Enable voice search for commodities.
* Support offline viewing through local on-device caching.
* Diagnose app crashes and improve app performance.

We do **not** sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data. We do **not** engage in user profiling, behavioral tracking, or targeted advertising.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

* **Ephemeral Data:** Location and voice inputs are processed temporarily in-memory and are not retained on our servers.
* **Local Data:** All watchlists, alerts, cached prices, and preferences remain solely on your device.
* **User Control & Deletion:** You can delete all locally stored data at any time by:
  1. Opening your device's **Settings** > **Apps** > **AgriMarket Pro**.
  2. Selecting **Storage & Cache** > **Clear Storage / Clear Data**.
  3. Or simply uninstalling the application.

---

## 6. Permissions Management

You can grant, deny, or revoke permissions (Location, Microphone) at any time through your Android device settings:
* Go to **Settings** > **Apps** > **AgriMarket Pro** > **Permissions**.
* If you decline location permission, AgriMarket Pro remains fully functional; you may select your mandi or region manually.

## 7. Children's Privacy

AgriMarket Pro is an agricultural utility tool designed for farmers, traders, and agricultural market participants. The application is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

We prioritize the security of your data. Network communications for market data, weather advisories, and backend synchronization are conducted over secure, encrypted channels (HTTPS/TLS). While no electronic transmission over the internet is 100% secure, we implement standard industry practices to protect your data.
