## 1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to **JBL Heritage** (also referred to as **Lansing Heritage**). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user privacy and data protection.

We believe that your personal data belongs to you. The Application is designed with a **privacy-first, offline-first philosophy**:
- **Zero Personal Data Collection**: We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal identifiable information (PII).
- **No User Tracking or Analytics**: We do not use remote analytics SDKs, telemetry, crash trackers, or advertising identifiers.
- **100% Offline Capability**: All catalogs, speaker specifications, crossover schematics, vector diagrams, and calculator engines run locally on your device without communicating with external servers.

## 3. Local On-Device Storage (`SharedPreferences`)

The Application utilizes standard local key-value storage (`SharedPreferences` on Android, local app storage on Windows/Web) solely to enhance your local user experience. 

### Data Stored Locally on Your Device:
1. **User Interface Preferences**:
   - `jbl_heritage_dark_mode`: Stores your selected display mode (`true` for Dark Studio Theme, `false` for Mid-Century Light Theme).
2. **Archival Favorites**:
   - `jbl_favorite_speakers`: List of speaker IDs you have bookmarked for quick access (e.g., `l100_century`, `paragon_d44000`).
3. **Comparison Tray State**:
   - Active speaker IDs selected for side-by-side acoustic comparison.
4. **Gear Vault Records**:
   - User-entered speaker inventory notes, serial numbers, acquisition dates, and restoration condition logs.

### Storage Security & Retention:
- **Exclusively Local**: All stored preferences and Gear Vault records remain strictly within the Application's sandboxed local container on your device.
- **Zero Cloud Transmission**: This data is **never** uploaded to external servers, cloud storage, or third-party databases.
- **Full User Control**: You can delete this data at any time by selecting "Clear Storage" in the application settings or by clearing the application's storage in your device operating system settings. Uninstalling the Application permanently removes all locally stored data.
